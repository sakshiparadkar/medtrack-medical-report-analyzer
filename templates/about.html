About MedTrack

From Medical Reports to Meaningful Health Insights

MedTrack is a health analytics web application that extracts information from laboratory report PDFs, processes test values, analyzes their trends, and presents the results in a clear and understandable format.

01

Upload PDF

Start by uploading your medical laboratory report in PDF format. MedTrack accepts your report and prepares it for automated processing.

📄 PDF Input
02

OCR Text Extraction

The uploaded report is scanned and its readable content is extracted automatically. Important text, test names and numerical values are identified from the document.

🔍 OCR Text Extraction
03

NLP & Feature Extraction

MedTrack processes the extracted text to identify medical terms, test names, measurements and relevant health information from the report.

🧠 NLP · Feature Extraction
04

PDF Processing

The extracted document content is processed and cleaned so that useful information can be separated from unnecessary formatting and document noise.

⚙️ PDF Processing
05

Structured Health Data

Extracted information is converted into organized health data. Test names, results and units can then be compared and analyzed consistently across reports.

📋 Structured Data
06

Health Trend Analysis

MedTrack organizes test results over time and visualizes their movement through charts, making it easier to notice changes and understand your health history.

📈 Trend Analysis · Plotly
07

Insights & Final Report

Finally, MedTrack presents the processed information through easy-to-understand reports, health trends and highlighted values, helping users follow their results more easily.

💡 Insights · Reports
