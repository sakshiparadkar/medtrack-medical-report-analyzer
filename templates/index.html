How it works

From PDF to insight in five steps

Scroll to follow one report through the pipeline.

1

Upload

Drop in a digital PDF lab report. No scanning or manual typing needed.

2

Extract

pdfplumber pulls the raw text, then regex and a reference-list matcher find each test name, value, unit and range.

3

Validate

Every value is checked against its reference range and flagged normal, abnormal, or needs review.

4

Store

Structured results are saved to a SQLite database, linked to your account and report date.

5

Visualize

Interactive Plotly charts and comparisons show how your numbers move over time.

Features

Everything in one place

From the first upload to a summary you can share with your doctor.

Automatic extraction

Test name, value, unit and range are pulled from your PDF with rule-based matching. No typing, and every result is explainable.

Clear severity levels

Values outside their range are marked yellow, orange or red depending on how far they are, so the important ones stand out at a glance.

Trends and alerts

See how each test moves across reports, spot values that keep rising, and get a notice when a value is outside your usual pattern.

Compare and timeline

Put two reports side by side, or scroll a timeline that shows what improved and what got worse since the last report.

Goals and health score

Set a target like "sugar below 100" and watch a progress bar fill up, with one simple score for your latest report.

Private and exportable

Reports are linked to your account and visible only after you sign in. Export a clean one-page PDF summary any time.

Trends

See the trend, not just the number

One number tells you little. Five reports tell a story. MedTrack plots every test over time against its normal range, so a slow climb is obvious long before it becomes a problem.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which reports are supported?

Digital (text-based) PDF lab reports. Scanned reports and photos are on the roadmap through OCR.

How are values marked abnormal?

Each value is compared with the reference range printed in the report or with MedTrack's reference list, then flagged Normal, Abnormal, or Needs Review.

What does "Needs Review" mean?

The value or range couldn't be read with confidence, so MedTrack asks you to double-check it instead of guessing.

Can I use it without an account?

The health quiz is public. Uploading and tracking reports needs a free account so your data stays yours.

Is this a replacement for a doctor?

No. MedTrack helps you organize and understand your numbers; medical decisions should always involve a professional.
